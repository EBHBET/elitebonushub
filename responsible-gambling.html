Responsible Gambling

Gambling should be entertainment — never a way to make money or escape problems. If it stops being fun, help is available, free and confidential, 24/7.

Stay in control

  • Set a budget before you play and never chase losses.
  • Only gamble with money you can afford to lose.
  • Set deposit, loss and time limits — most crypto casinos offer them.
  • Take regular breaks; never gamble under the influence or to cope with stress.
  • Don't see gambling as income. The house always has an edge.

Warning signs

Spending more than you can afford, chasing losses, borrowing to gamble, lying about it, or feeling anxious when not playing — these are signs to seek help.

Get help now (free & confidential)

  • BeGambleAware.org — advice and support
  • GamCare — free 24/7 helpline
  • GamblingTherapy.org — global multilingual support
  • NCPG — call/text 1-800-522-4700 (US)

You can also self-exclude through tools like GAMSTOP, or set self-exclusion directly with each casino. You must be 18+ (or the legal age in your jurisdiction) to gamble.
