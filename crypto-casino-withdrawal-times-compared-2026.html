Two Different Clocks: Casino Approval vs. Blockchain Confirmation

Every figure above is really two numbers stacked together. First, the casino has to internally approve the withdrawal — this can be near-instant for automated systems or take hours if a manual or KYC review is triggered (often past a size threshold, as RamenBet's publicly documented $1,000 review tier illustrates). Second, once released, the transaction still has to confirm on whichever blockchain network was used — and that step is identical across every casino using the same network. A TRC-20 USDT transfer confirms in roughly the same 1–5 minutes whether it's leaving Gamdom or BC.Game; a native Bitcoin withdrawal takes roughly the same 10–60 minutes regardless of casino. FAQ

Did EliteBonusHub test these withdrawal times itself?

No. This page compiles publicly stated processing times from each casino's own help center where available, and from independent review/tracking sites where a casino doesn't publish a specific figure. Every row states which type of source it draws from.

Why do some rows say "third-party" instead of an official source?

Not every casino publishes a specific withdrawal-time figure on its own help center. Where that's the case, we cite independent review or tracking sites instead and flag it clearly rather than presenting it as the casino's own claim.

Why do crypto withdrawal times vary so much even at the same casino?

Two separate steps are involved: how fast the casino approves and releases the withdrawal internally, and how fast the blockchain network itself confirms the transaction. Verification status, withdrawal size, and network congestion all affect the first step; the coin and network chosen (e.g. TRC-20 versus native Bitcoin) affect the second.

Which casinos publish the clearest withdrawal-time information?

Of the casinos checked, Stake, Gamdom, Cloudbet and Metaspins had the clearest first-party help-center documentation at the time of research. Several others only had specific figures available from third-party tracking sites, not their own official pages.
