# The iGaming Authority Engine — Backlinks, Digital PR & Off-Page SEO

**A complete strategy guide, synthesized from four creator videos (three creators) into one framework, a step-by-step tutorial, and an automation spec.**
Compiled for EliteBonusHub. Last updated: 2026-07-02.

> ⚠️ **Knowledge / research note.** This guide documents the *full* off-page playbook used in the iGaming affiliate industry — **including paid link buying and link swaps** — for completeness and study. Buying links, link exchanges, and PBNs **violate Google's Webmaster/Spam guidelines** and carry real penalty risk; every creator analyzed says so openly. It is recorded here as industry knowledge. For EliteBonusHub, the operator (you) decides what to execute. The automated/agent work in this project is **restricted to the safe, guideline-compliant areas** (linkable assets, digital-PR drafting, list-building, tracking, competitor analysis, guest-post drafting for you to place). Nothing in the "paid" pillar is executed automatically.

## 1. Source analysis (what each creator contributes)

**Source A — casino-affiliate operator (Videos 1 & 2): the paid-link engine.**
In iGaming you can't earn links at scale — everyone buys, and it's the biggest business cost. Safety comes from **profile naturalness**, not restraint on volume: anchor-text distribution, velocity matched to traffic, dofollow/nofollow mix, topical + geographic diversity, homepage bias. Vet on **Domain Rating + organic traffic**; do an "ocular" real-site check; avoid spammy daily-guest-post sites. Run a **tracking sheet** as the control system. Source inventory from trusted sellers (don't do your own cold outreach). Acquisition types: guest posts, **A→B→C swaps** (never reciprocal A↔B), **testimonial links** from casinos you rank, **operator footer-logo** links. Avoid: Fiverr bulk links, PBNs, and — #1 penalty cause — **over-optimized exact-match anchors**. Pay via PayPal; never before the link is live.

**Source B — John Wright / Olga Zarr podcast (Video 3): the strategic shift.**
Post-Helpful-Content, **content-only comparison/info sites are being de-indexed**. Winners are **product-led**: forums/UGC, complaints/arbitration (AskGamblers, CasinoMeister — acquired at big multiples), **awards**, **unique data stories**, tools/recommendation engines that earn their own links. Content must be **bottom-of-funnel and genuinely deep** (Surfer = average; Cora / AI-persona prompting = beyond competitors). **Digital PR** (Search Intelligence / Ferry Casoni style) is the durable future. **PBNs are effectively dead** — link power = the linking page's real authority/EEAT, not IP/design. Diversify **languages/markets** (French, Polish, Serbian, Finnish, LatAm), **channels** (podcast/streaming/forums), and add **local-entity + hands-on-review E-E-A-T** signals. Penalties are hard to undo; big players survive via bankroll/consolidation.

**Source C — Adam Enfroy (Video 4): AI-industrialized outreach (cross-niche).**
The operating layer that scales B's PR and A's guest posts. Highest-value links come from **the brands/companies in your niche** (the ones you'd be an affiliate for), not random blogs. Machine: (1) list-build 50–100 targets (ChatGPT / Harpa); (2) enrich via LinkedIn + Hunter.io (or Apollo); (3) outreach = one-sentence **value proposition + incentive** (offer links back); (4) AI-draft guest posts (Claude prose, ChatGPT research), **humanize/fact-check**, place your link high (first 8–10 paragraphs) + bio link, ~1 link/200 words, link out to partners. It's **relationships + value exchange**; keep a **link surplus** (~5 guest posts always in progress). Plus **HARO** and **broken-link** templates.

**Consensus:** links must largely be bought in iGaming; **quality > quantity**; the profile must look natural; reverse-engineer competitors; it's relationships + value exchange; over-optimization = penalty.
**Evolution:** A optimizes the paid machine → B says the machine alone now sinks you without a **product/linkable-asset + diversification** layer → C shows how **AI compresses the labor** of both.

## 3. Step-by-step tutorial (human playbook)

### Step 0 — Guardrails (Pillar 1)

**Anchor-text distribution** (per target page; track running %):

| Anchor type | Target share | Example (EliteBonusHub) |
|---|---|---|
| Branded | 40–50% | "EliteBonusHub", "EliteBonusHub.bet" |
| Naked URL | 15–20% | `elitebonushub.bet`, full https URL |
| Generic / CTA / image | 10–15% | "read more", "click here", logo image |
| Partial / semi-match | 15–20% | "bonus guide at EliteBonusHub" |
| **Exact match** | **5–10% max — ~1 per page** | "crypto casino bonuses" |

**Velocity ladder:**

| Stage | Organic visits/day | Links/week |
|---|---|---|
| New, week 1 | ~0 | 1–2 total, then ~1/wk |
| Month 1 | 30–50 | ~3/wk |
| Growth | 100+ | scale; ~10–15/mo/site |

**Follow mix:** majority dofollow, deliberately sprinkle nofollow. **Homepage bias:** most links → homepage. **Safety:** PayPal; never pay before live.

### Step 1 — Tracking sheet (Pillar 7)
Columns: `Live backlink URL | Target URL | Anchor text | Anchor type | Article subject | Host site | DR | Traffic | Follow | Date live | Price | Seller/Vendor | Content writer | Status(ordered→ready→published→paid)`. Add an **anchor-% calculator** + **monthly velocity & spend total**. One tab per site.

### Step 2 — Paid-link engine (Pillar 2) — *documented for study; execute at your discretion*
1. **Source** inventory from vetted sellers (Upwork gigs by target country; or a link marketplace for one-invoice scale) — not your own cold outreach.
2. **Vet** (buy only if it passes): healthy DR **and** real organic traffic; steady traffic trend (not spike-then-flat); not 10 guest posts/day; niche/geo relevant *or* chosen for diversity; real-looking; allows casino; check IP diversity.
3. **Buy**, negotiate (esp. multi-buy), log, mark paid only once live.
4. **Cheaper/earned types in parallel:** testimonial links from casinos you rank; operator **footer-logo** links; **A→B→C swaps** across sites you control (never reciprocal).
5. **Avoid:** Fiverr bulk, PBNs, over-optimized exact anchors, excessive reciprocal exchange.

### Step 3 — Competitor reverse-engineering (Pillar 5)
Ahrefs → competitor in Site Explorer → **Referring domains** (not raw backlinks) → sort by DR/traffic → list strongest replicable links → replicate.

### Step 4 — Linkable-asset / product layer (Pillar 3) — *durability play*
- **Awards** ("Best Crypto Casino Bonuses 2026") → winners embed a badge → backlinks + press release.
- **Unique data study** (e.g., "We timed withdrawals at 25 crypto casinos") → journalists/affiliates cite it.
- **Tool / recommendation engine** (bonus finder, wagering-requirement calculator).
- **Hands-on documented reviews** (real deposit/games/screenshots) — the E-E-A-T format that survives.
- Heavy moat: complaints/arbitration or forum/UGC.

### Step 5 — AI outreach & content pipeline (Pillar 4)
1. List-build 50–100 targets (AI + Harpa).
2. Enrich (LinkedIn + Hunter.io / Apollo).
3. Outreach = value prop + incentive; personalize first line; follow up (email + LinkedIn); >5% reply = good.
4. Guest posts: pitch 3 topics (helps their audience / doesn't exist yet / SEO value) → AI draft → **humanize & fact-check** → link high + bio; ~1 link/200 words; link out to partners.
5. HARO + broken-link as steady supplements.
6. Keep a **link surplus** (~5 in progress).

### Step 6 — Diversification (Pillar 6)
Add a thinner **second language/market** (this project ships **French + Spanish** versions); add **local-entity** signals; write **bottom-of-funnel depth** competitors won't; spread **channels**; keep responsible-gambling/ethics real.

### Step 7 — Governance (Pillar 7)
Monthly: recompute anchor %, confirm velocity ≤ traffic-justified, review spend vs ranking movement, prune weak links, keep the profile **sellable**.

## 6. Glossary
- **DR** — Domain Rating (Ahrefs authority metric, 0–100).
- **Dofollow / nofollow** — whether a link passes ranking equity ("link juice").
- **Anchor text** — the clickable text of a link.
- **Velocity** — rate of new links over time.
- **PBN** — private blog network (owned sites built only to link out; high risk).
- **Digital PR** — earning editorial links via newsworthy assets (data, awards, stories).
- **Bottom-of-funnel** — content for buyers ready to act, not top-of-funnel definitions.
- **E-E-A-T** — Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust.
