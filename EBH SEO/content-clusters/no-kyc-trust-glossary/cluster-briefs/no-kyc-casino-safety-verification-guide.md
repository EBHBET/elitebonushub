### Suggested outline

1. **Quick Verdict** (50-70 words) — Most "no-KYC" casinos are actually "no-KYC-until-triggered." Full anonymity with zero verification ever is rare and, counterintuitively, more often a red flag than a feature — legitimate operators still need to comply with anti-fraud rules once certain thresholds are hit.
2. **H2: Full No-KYC vs. Partial-KYC — the real distinction** — most sites are partial: no ID at signup or for small withdrawals, but ID required past a threshold (commonly $5,000+).
3. **H2: The 4 common triggers for a verification request** — large withdrawal amount, unusual/rapid betting patterns, multiple accounts linked to one player, a specific regulatory/AML flag.
4. **H2: What verification actually involves when it happens** — ID + proof of address submission, automated or compliance-team review, typical turnaround (minutes to several days).
5. **H2: How to vet a no-KYC site before you deposit** — checklist: verifiable license (Curaçao/Anjouan registry lookup), public reserve/audit information if available, provably-fair game verification, independent community reputation (Trustpilot, forums — not just the casino's own claims).
6. **H2: Red flags** — no license info at all, no way to verify game fairness, opaque or shifting withdrawal terms, no responsible-gambling resources.
7. **FAQ (schema-marked, 4 Q&As):**
   - Can a no-KYC casino refuse to pay me?
   - What happens if I refuse to verify when asked?
