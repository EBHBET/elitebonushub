Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 June 2026. This page explains what limited data we collect and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Analytics: We use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate traffic (pages viewed, country, device, referrer). IP addresses are anonymised. We do not identify you personally.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, run our own ad tracking, or collect financial information. We never receive your casino account details, deposits or balances — those stay with the operator.

Third parties

Data is processed by Google (Analytics) and Mailchimp (email) under their own privacy policies. Partner casinos you visit have their own policies once you leave our site.

Your rights

You can request access to, or deletion of, any email data we hold by contacting us via the Contact page.
